### Planning Poker — Goal

#### Outcome

Become the highest-volume app in the suite — the one that brings people into
everything else.

#### Why this app can win it

Estimation happens weekly and pulls a whole team into the same URL at the same
moment, which makes it the suite's best distribution surface. The category is
crowded with free tools, so the win cannot come from having cards — it comes from
zero friction and from being the only estimator that knows about the team's other
data.

#### Signals

1. Sessions run, and participants per session.
2. Share of participants who later open another app in the suite.
3. Teams returning for a second and tenth session.
4. GitHub stars and inbound links.

#### Boundaries

- Not a backlog or issue tracker.
- No mandatory signup — a session must be joinable from a link alone.
- Not locked to one estimation scale.

*Repo: `agile-toolkit/planning-poker`*

---

## Latitude

Every goal above names an outcome, not a mechanism. Any roadmap that moves a
signal without crossing a boundary is fair game — including approaches nobody
in this category has tried. Invention is expected, not merely permitted.
